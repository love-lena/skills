# Fancy

Fancy means a deliberate approach to organizing information and interaction, expressed through composition, palette, typography, density, shape, and depth. The approved examples differ in how the reader finds meaning and acts on it.

## Choose one approach

| Approach | Organizing unit | Guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Register · cobalt worksheet | Repeated records, shared axes, visible exceptions | [Register](styles/register.md) |
| Annotated editorial · warm working document | A continuous argument with evidence attached in place | [Editorial](styles/editorial.md) |

## Compose from the information

Each approach owns its entry point, proportions, grouping, empty space, and headline role. A register can begin directly with records; an editorial page can arrive at its conclusion after the evidence; a canvas can identify itself through labels on the object. Decide these things for the content. A universal title/main-panel/footer scaffold would erase the differences.

The palettes in the guides are concrete starting points. Preserve their character unless the user requests another palette; adapt contrast, density, and sizing to the output. Appearance toggles are optional in Fancy, and every appearance offered must be designed and checked.

Build directly in HTML/CSS/SVG with the chosen composition. The Plain renderer is for Plain artifacts. For working applications, implement actual controls with semantic elements and behavior; the approved static examples illustrate composition, not functionality.

The examples use one control-program proposal for calibration. Their organizations, uncertainties, and domain names belong to that example only. Transfer the design decisions to the task's facts. When comparing alternatives, keep supplied facts and material limits consistent; make any important content omitted for focus discoverable or explain the omission.

The next guides show applications to KPIs, design feedback, fast forms, and explanations. These are uses of the same approach, not additional mandatory layouts.
