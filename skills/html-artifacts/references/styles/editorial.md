# Annotated editorial · warm working document

A continuous reading path is the organizing unit. Evidence and commentary attach to the passages or figure locations they explain.

## Composition

Give the argument room to unfold. Use a principal reading surface with shorter annotations tied to specific points. A headline can introduce the question or arrive as the conclusion after the evidence; choose its role from the reading order. Vary scale and spacing according to the argument instead of making every section an equal card.

The approved example is tall (1080 × 1480). Its large conclusion follows the sequence. That placement expresses this argument, not a required bottom-title rule. On narrow screens, move annotations immediately after their anchors.

## Visual language

Warm paper `#F6F0E4`, ink `#332C3C`, terracotta `#B35034`, rules `#CBBBA7`. Use a serif editorial voice with sans-serif metadata and, when useful, a monospace specimen. Fine rules and a restrained tint connect evidence; quiet rectangular regions and no decorative elevation keep the page continuous.

## Apply it

- **KPIs:** an authored finding with a directly labeled small comparison and a source or caveat at the claim.
- **Design feedback:** margin comments anchored to a passage, screenshot detail, or proposed decision.
- **Fast forms:** inline sentence completion when the sentence meaning clarifies the input; use visible labels and conventional controls.
- **Explainers:** a sequence with branches for reuse, evidence, or implications encountered at the relevant moment.

Choose this for an authored reading experience. It trades arbitrary scanning and repeated bulk operations for context and interpretation. Keep the annotation relationship clear when it reflows.
